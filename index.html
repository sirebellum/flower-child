Mathematical & Sacred Geometry Specification

1. The Core Geometric Distinction: Hexagon vs. Circle

Both geometries share the foundational base circle radius r and expanded petal radius R = 2r, with layer vertex distance d_k = 2kr. The profound mathematical divergence arises in how intermediary circle centers are generated across layers:

2. Why 19 vs. 18 Circles?

In traditional sacred geometry, the Flower of Life is a closed hexagonal loop with 19 complete circles enclosed by a double outer ring. The central circle at (0,0) acts as the genesis seed.

In Krystonial / Kathara mechanics, the Flower of Kryst omits the central circle. Instead, the 6 circles of Layer 1 have radius 2r and are centered at distance 2r from the origin; thus, all 6 circles naturally pass directly through the origin (0,0), forming the 6-petal core meeting at a pure zero-point singularity. Layer 2 then places its 12 circles evenly around a smooth 360° circle, allowing open-ended spherical expansion rather than finite hexagonal collapse.

3. Hexagon 2 Non-Overlapping Inscription Proof

In classical linear spacing (d_k = 2kr), Hexagon 2 is placed at d_3 = 6r. Because a regular hexagon's edge midpoints lie at distance 6r × √3/2 ≈ 5.196r, base circles of radius r reach inward to 4.196r, which deeply overlaps Circle 1's base circles (extending out to 4r + r = 5.0r). Furthermore, Hexagon 2 floats inside Circle 2 (radius 8r) without touching it.

In the Inscribed Harmonic Matrix (Default), Hexagon 2's circumradius is set to d_3 = 8r, exactly matching Circle 2:

  • Fully Enveloped & Touched: All 6 vertices of Hexagon 2 touch Circle 2 (radius 8r) exactly, and the entire hexagon is enclosed inside Circle 2.
  • Zero Overlap: Hexagon 2's closest point to origin is its edge midpoint at 8r × √3/2 ≈ 6.928r. Base circles extend inward to 6.928r - r = 5.928r, which sits cleanly outside Circle 1's base circles (max extent 5.0r), leaving a clear separation gap of ≈ 0.928r (over 46px at base r=50).

4. Embedded Platonic Solids (Metatron's Cube)

By taking the 13 tangent circles of the Fruit of Life (1 center + 6 layer 1 + 6 layer 2 vertices) and connecting all 13 centers to one another via straight line segments (C(13,2) = 78 lines), all five Platonic solids (Tetrahedron, Cube, Octahedron, Icosahedron, Dodecahedron) are geometrically inscribed in three dimensions.
