Mathematical & Sacred Geometry Specification

1. The Core Geometric Distinction: Hexagon vs. Circle

Both geometries share the foundational base circle radius r and expanded petal radius R = 2r, with layer vertex distance d_k = 2kr. The profound mathematical divergence arises in how intermediary circle centers are generated across layers:

Property Flower of Life (Hexagonal Matrix) Flower of Kryst (Krystonial Matrix)
Layer 0 (Origin) 1 Center at (0, 0) 0 Centers (Empty Zero-Point Origin)
Innermost Hexagon 6 centers at radius 2r (angles m × 60°) 6 centers at radius 2r (angles m × 60°), surrounding empty center at (0, 0)
Surrounding Triangles Concentric hexagonal perimeter rings 6 Triangles × 3 circles (18 circles): Base midpoint at (2+√3)r ≈ 3.732r, Apex at (2+2√3)r ≈ 5.464r
Outer Ring of 6 Hexagons Hexagonal perimeter progression 6 Hexagons × 7 circles (42 ring + 6 center circles): Centers at (4+2√3)r ≈ 7.464r along 30° axes + 6 Bridge Triangles (18 circles)
Stamp Expansion (N ≥ 2) Concentric hexagonal perimeter rings Hexagon Lattice Restamping: Stamp translates outward to each outer hexagon center, strictly preserving the empty origin at (0, 0)
Tangency & Alignment Overlapping 2r radius circles Strict Tangency (Distance = 2r): Zero overlaps and zero gaps between all adjacent base circles

2. Why 19 vs. 18 Circles & The Zero-Point Center

In traditional sacred geometry, the Flower of Life is a closed hexagonal loop with 19 complete circles enclosed by a double outer ring. The central circle at (0,0) acts as the genesis seed.

In Krystonial mechanics, the Flower of Kryst omits the central circle at (0,0). Instead, the 6 circles of the innermost hexagon have radius 2r and are centered at distance 2r from the origin; thus, all 6 circles naturally pass directly through the origin (0,0), forming the 6-petal core meeting at a pure zero-point singularity. Every other hexagon outside (0,0) contains a center circle.

3. Hexagon-and-Triangle Stamp Pattern Mathematics

The authoritative Flower of Kryst geometry is generated by a single cohesive Stamp constructed from nested 6-circle Hexagons and 3-circle Triangles:

  • Innermost Hexagon: 6 circles at distance 2r along m × 60° axes. Only hexagons away from the global origin (0,0) receive a center circle.
  • Inner Triangles (6 × 3 = 18 circles): Along each m × 60° axis, an inner pair at distance (2 + √3)r ≈ 3.732r ± r tangent, plus an apex circle at distance (2 + 2√3)r ≈ 5.464r.
  • Outer Hexagons (6 × 7 = 42 ring + 6 center circles): Centers positioned at distance (4 + 2√3)r ≈ 7.464r along 30° + m × 60° rays. Each outer hexagon has its center circle plus 6 ring circles of radius 2r.
  • Bridge Triangles (6 × 3 = 18 circles): Outward-facing 3-circle triangles placed between adjacent outer hexagons along m × 60° rays, perfectly touching the inner triangle apex circles.
  • Iterative Restamping (N ≥ 2): Breadth-first translation outward moves the stamp onto each outer hexagon center, seamlessly tiling the lattice while keeping (0,0) as the only hexagon without a center circle.

4. Embedded Platonic Solids (Metatron's Cube)

By taking the 13 tangent circles of the Fruit of Life (1 center + 6 layer 1 + 6 layer 2 vertices) and connecting all 13 centers to one another via straight line segments (C(13,2) = 78 lines), all five Platonic solids (Tetrahedron, Cube, Octahedron, Icosahedron, Dodecahedron) are geometrically inscribed in three dimensions.
